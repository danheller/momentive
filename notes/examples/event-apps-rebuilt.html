Features

Bring your events to life and boost attendee engagement.

Optimize the event experience for attendees and staff with powerful features and capabilities.

Build community through networking and engagement.

Help attendees make valuable connections and meet the right people before, during, and after the event with networking tools like 1:1 connections, messages, meetings, and matchmaking. With permission-based connections, attendees are in control of their experience. Keep your attendees updated and engaged with content, exhibitors, and activities through push notifications, social media feed, surveys, and polls.

Create one-of-a-kind, personalized experiences.

Empower attendees to create their own agendas to fit their interests. Registrants can sort sessions by date and time and can easily add sessions and activities to their personal schedule. The next upcoming item is always available on the dashboard for an attendee to easily remember where they need to be next.

Drive sponsorship revenue  & ROI.

Maximize your sponsors’ ROI with a variety of sponsor highlights, including sponsor lists, exhibitor profiles, ads, push notifications, and more. Monetize and underwrite the cost of your event app by selling sponsorship perks like push notifications or banner ads to your exhibitors and industry partners.

Offer attendees interactive navigation tools.

Ensure your attendees always know where to be with interactive floor plans and maps that help them get to their next session, upcoming meeting, or a must-see exhibitor. Attendees can get the best path routing booth-to-booth, to and from session rooms, and more.

Monitor event performance.

Use a robust stats portal for all your app analytics—from attendee engagement to post-event survey feedback—to measure your event’s success. Post-event analytics will help you make informed decisions for your next event, leverage engagement data to personalize your outreach efforts, and convert first-time attendees into returning attendees.
